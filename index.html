What the numbers say

  • At 100k emails a month the published bills run from $10 (Amazon SES) to $115 (Postmark).
  • At 10k a month, SMTP2GO ($10) is the cheapest managed API after SES ($1).
  • At 1M a month the cheapest managed API is MailerSend at $600, against $100 on SES a la carte.
  • New SES accounts now start on the Essentials plan at $0.16 per 1,000, 60% above the $0.10 a la carte rate, unless the account switches.
