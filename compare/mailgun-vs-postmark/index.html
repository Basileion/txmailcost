Mailgun

Free: Free: 100 emails a day, 1 custom domain, 1 day of logs.

  • Entry tiers only (Basic 10k, Foundation 50k, Scale 100k). Larger tiers sit behind the volume slider and were not readable, so larger volumes use the entry tier plus its published overage rate and may overstate cost.
  • Foundation and Scale are free for the first month.
  • Extra dedicated IPs are $59/IP/mo; one is included with Foundation 100k, Growth and Scale.

Postmark

Free: Free developer plan: 100 emails a month, no overages, never expires.

  • Tiers read from the pricing calculator on postmarkapp.com (Basic, Pro, Platform at 10k, 50k, 125k, 300k, 700k and 1.5M).
  • Dedicated IPs start at $50/mo per IP, for senders of 300,000+ emails a month on Pro or higher.
  • Bootstrapped companies can ask support for $75 in account credit (per the pricing FAQ).
