Amazon SES

Free: No permanent free tier. New AWS customers get up to $200 in Free Tier credits that can apply to SES (per the SES pricing page).

  • Since Jul 21, 2026, new SES accounts (and account-region pairs with no metered SES use since Jun 1, 2025) start on the Essentials plan at $0.16 per 1,000 emails. A la carte pricing is $0.10 per 1,000 and can be chosen at any time.
  • Attachment data is billed at $0.12 per GB on top of the per-email rate.
  • Pro plan adds a $105 per account per region monthly fee; Enterprise adds $500.
