Amazon SES now defaults new accounts to a 60% higher rate

The SES pricing page says new accounts, and account-region pairs with no metered SES activity since Jun 1, 2025, start on the Essentials plan beginning Jul 21, 2026. Essentials charges $0.16 per 1,000 emails for the first 10M. A la carte pricing is $0.10 per 1,000.

What Essentials adds

The pricing page describes Essentials as a plan to "monitor how emails perform and get insights to help improve deliverability over time." On a la carte, the page lists Virtual Deliverability Manager separately at $0.07 per 1,000 emails for the first 10M.

Switching

The page states you can switch to a la carte at any time, and that if you were defaulted to Essentials without choosing it, your first downgrade or cancellation to a la carte takes effect immediately.
