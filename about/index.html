Methodology

  • Prices are read from each provider's public pricing page (or the calculator data that page loads) and stored with the source URL and check date.
  • For a volume, each plan's bill is base price if the volume fits its quota, otherwise base plus published overage per 1,000. The cheapest plan per provider is shown.
  • Plans with no published overage (free tiers, Mailtrap tiers) only count up to their quota.
  • Where a provider shows only entry tiers as text (SendGrid, Mailgun, SMTP2GO) the bill is entry tier plus overage and is marked † as a possible overstatement.
  • Monthly billing, USD, before tax. Annual discounts, credits and dedicated IPs are noted per provider but not included.
  • No numbers are estimated or invented. If a price could not be read, it is left out.
